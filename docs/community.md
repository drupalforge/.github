# Get involved

[Documentation home](index.md)

## Volunteer

Drupal Forge relies on volunteers to move it forward.

1. Join [#drupalforge](https://drupal.slack.com/archives/C077WUMH7P1) on Drupal
   Slack and introduce yourself. Drupal Slack membership is required.
2. Join the weekly [volunteer meeting](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1JP_2j7YfBADT4nPmfxKL71jSVpZ0_nNLNZIKFP-6U0I)
   and add items to the agenda.
3. Pick or propose work in the [issue queue](https://www.drupal.org/project/issues/drupalforge)
   or explore the [road map](https://www.drupal.org/docs/getting-started/sustainability/sustainable-contribution/drupal-forge/drupal-forge-road-map).

| Need | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Discuss work or ask for help | [#drupalforge](https://drupal.slack.com/archives/C077WUMH7P1) |
| Plan work and make decisions together | Weekly [volunteer meeting](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1JP_2j7YfBADT4nPmfxKL71jSVpZ0_nNLNZIKFP-6U0I) |
| Track or propose project work | [Drupal.org issue queue](https://www.drupal.org/project/issues/drupalforge) |
| Report a repository or workflow problem | An issue in that repository |

## Sell site templates

Follow the [template setup guide](template-setup.md). To sell ready-made Drupal
sites and earn recurring revenue, [open a vendor issue](https://www.drupal.org/node/add/project-issue/drupalforge?categories=task&component=Vendors)
with the _Vendors_ component.

## Donate or sponsor

Free demos and the cloud development environment rely on donors.

- [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/drupalforge): support Drupal Forge.
- To discuss sponsorship, such as hosting specific demos or promoting a service
  that adds value to Drupal, contact a maintainer listed on the
  [project page](https://www.drupal.org/project/drupalforge).
- [OPS Association public documents](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1uH7UwCzjKlior_Kf53Jv_UE2sPkxsJC-):
  public records from Drupal Forge's fiscal host.

## Partner

Hosting providers, agencies, and other organizations can work with Drupal Forge
to reach site owners. Start a conversation in
[#drupalforge](https://drupal.slack.com/archives/C077WUMH7P1) or contact a
maintainer listed on the [project page](https://www.drupal.org/project/drupalforge).
