[Drupal Forge](https://www.drupalforge.org/) is a volunteer-driven model
marketplace for ready-made Drupal sites that is designed to sustain open
contribution and collaboration.

- [Website](https://www.drupalforge.org/): browse site templates and launch a
  free demo in seconds.
- [Project page](https://www.drupal.org/project/drupalforge): learn how Drupal
  Forge works and why.
- [Road map](https://www.drupal.org/docs/getting-started/sustainability/sustainable-contribution/drupal-forge/drupal-forge-road-map):
  explore planned work.

## Build a site template

Sell ready-made Drupal sites and earn recurring revenue.

- [Starter template](https://github.com/drupalforge/starter_template): create a
  Composer-managed Drupal Forge site template.
- [Docker publish action](https://github.com/drupalforge/docker_publish_action):
  build and publish template images.
- [Become a vendor](https://www.drupal.org/node/add/project-issue/drupalforge?categories=task&component=Vendors):
  open an issue with the _Vendors_ component to sell sites through Drupal Forge.
- Ask for help in [#drupalforge](https://drupal.slack.com/archives/C077WUMH7P1)
  on Drupal Slack.

## Volunteer

Drupal Forge relies on volunteers to move it forward.

- [Issue queue](https://www.drupal.org/project/issues/drupalforge): find, discuss,
  and organize work.
- [Volunteer meeting agenda](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1JP_2j7YfBADT4nPmfxKL71jSVpZ0_nNLNZIKFP-6U0I):
  join the weekly volunteer meeting.
- [Drupal Slack: #drupalforge](https://drupal.slack.com/archives/C077WUMH7P1):
  connect with the community; Drupal Slack membership is required.
- [Road map](https://www.drupal.org/docs/getting-started/sustainability/sustainable-contribution/drupal-forge/drupal-forge-road-map):
  pick a task.

## Donate or sponsor

Free demos and the cloud development environment rely on donors.

- [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/drupalforge): donate and view the
  budget.
- Sponsors can donate at the sponsor level, fund hosting for specific demos, or
  promote a service that adds value to Drupal.
- [OPS Association public documents](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1uH7UwCzjKlior_Kf53Jv_UE2sPkxsJC-):
  public records from Drupal Forge’s fiscal host.

## Partner

Hosting providers, agencies, and other organizations can work with Drupal Forge
to reach site owners. Start a conversation in
[#drupalforge](https://drupal.slack.com/archives/C077WUMH7P1) or contact a
maintainer listed on the
[project page](https://www.drupal.org/project/drupalforge).
