[Drupal Forge](https://www.drupalforge.org/) is a volunteer-driven model
marketplace for ready-made Drupal sites that is designed to sustain open
contribution and collaboration.

- [Website](https://www.drupalforge.org/): browse site templates and launch a
  free demo in seconds.
- [Project page](https://www.drupal.org/project/drupalforge): learn how Drupal
  Forge works and why.
- [Road map](https://www.drupal.org/docs/getting-started/sustainability/sustainable-contribution/drupal-forge/drupal-forge-road-map):
  explore planned work.

## Build a site template

Sell ready-made Drupal sites and earn recurring revenue.

Follow the [template setup guide](https://github.com/drupalforge/starter_template/wiki)
for repository setup, quick start images, DevPanel configuration, and your
Drupal Forge template page. Sections marked **TODO** are incomplete.

To sell sites through Drupal Forge, [open a vendor issue](https://www.drupal.org/node/add/project-issue/drupalforge?categories=task&component=Vendors)
with the _Vendors_ component.

## Volunteer

Drupal Forge relies on volunteers to move it forward.

1. Join [#drupalforge](https://drupal.slack.com/archives/C077WUMH7P1) on Drupal
   Slack and introduce yourself. Drupal Slack membership is required.
2. Join the weekly [volunteer meeting](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1JP_2j7YfBADT4nPmfxKL71jSVpZ0_nNLNZIKFP-6U0I)
   to plan work, make decisions, and add items to the agenda.
3. Pick or propose work in the [issue queue](https://www.drupal.org/project/issues/drupalforge)
   or explore the [road map](https://www.drupal.org/docs/getting-started/sustainability/sustainable-contribution/drupal-forge/drupal-forge-road-map).
4. Optionally, [request GitHub organization membership or a directory listing](https://github.com/drupalforge/.github/issues/new?template=contributor-directory.yml)
   with a public issue. Membership and the
   [contributor directory](https://github.com/drupalforge/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTORS.md)
   are independent.

For questions or documentation corrections, ask in Slack. For a repository or
workflow problem, open an issue in that repository.

## Donate or sponsor

Free demos and the cloud development environment rely on donors.

- [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/drupalforge): support Drupal Forge.
- To discuss sponsorship, such as hosting specific demos or promoting a service
  that adds value to Drupal, contact a maintainer listed on the
  [project page](https://www.drupal.org/project/drupalforge).
- [OPS Association public documents](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1uH7UwCzjKlior_Kf53Jv_UE2sPkxsJC-):
  public records from Drupal Forge's fiscal host.

## Partner

Hosting providers, agencies, and other organizations can work with Drupal Forge
to reach site owners. Start a conversation in
[#drupalforge](https://drupal.slack.com/archives/C077WUMH7P1) or contact a
maintainer listed on the [project page](https://www.drupal.org/project/drupalforge).

## Press and brand

Use the canonical public resources rather than copying them into your repository:

- [Brand guidelines](https://github.com/drupalforge/.github/blob/main/assets/brand/README.md):
  name, logos, color, and typography.
- [Boilerplate](https://github.com/drupalforge/.github/blob/main/assets/brand/boilerplate.md):
  approved descriptions.
- [Press kit](https://github.com/drupalforge/.github/blob/main/assets/brand/press-kit.md):
  key facts, logo downloads, and media contacts.
- [Public Drupal Forge folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1MlQTpdy_5MnADbAUdF0RhzGBPUr6wZt_):
  public project materials.

Write **Drupal Forge**; use lowercase `drupalforge` only for machine names.
