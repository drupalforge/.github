Copy these descriptions when you need to introduce Drupal Forge. Follow the

## One line

Drupal Forge is a volunteer-driven model marketplace for ready-made Drupal sites
that is designed to sustain open contribution and collaboration.

## Short

Drupal Forge is a volunteer-driven model marketplace for ready-made Drupal
sites. Anyone can launch a free, disposable demo of a site template in seconds,
then save it to customize, buy a subscription, or deploy it to their own
hosting. Template authors earn recurring revenue, and partners share revenue
with the Drupal Association and the projects they rely on.

## Long

Drupal Forge is a volunteer-driven model marketplace for ready-made Drupal sites
that is designed to sustain open contribution and collaboration.

Visitors to Drupal Forge can launch disposable demos of site templates in
seconds and use them for evaluation or training. They can save a demo to
customize it, then buy a site subscription or deploy it to their own hosting.
Site owners get the convenience of a hosted platform with full ownership of
their sites. Each demo includes a cloud development environment, which also
gives contributors a cloud environment for working on Drupal.

Freelancers and agencies sell site subscriptions and earn recurring revenue,
hosting providers gain a sales channel, and Drupal gains a business model that
rewards contribution: partners are asked to share subscription revenue with the
Drupal Association and the Drupal projects their products rely on.

Drupal Forge is run by volunteers and funded by donors through
[Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/drupalforge), with OPS Association
as its fiscal host.
